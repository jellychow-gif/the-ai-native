## 1. One-paragraph product summary

**The AI Native** is an anonymously-operated weekly content site at
**https://the-ai-native.vercel.app** that publishes 8-9 structured intelligence signals every Friday 08:00 Beijing time about how frontier
companies redesign talent, coordination, and management in the AI era.
Target audience: CHROs, founders, HRDs at AI-native companies (Anthropic,
OpenAI, ServiceNow, Workday, Block, Tesla, Duolingo, YC). Editor is the
HRD of MokaHR but the site is operated **anonymously** to preserve
editorial freedom (PRD §1.2, §2.3, §9). Repositioned in v2 as
**"AI Native Organization Observatory"** — Bloomberg-Terminal-grade
intelligence platform, not news feed.

## 11. Anti-patterns / lessons learned

### Don't do these
- ❌ Surface items > 180 days old on the homepage / section pages / Top 5
  (Issue archive pages are the only allowed historical surface)
- ❌ Let one source dominate Top 5 (max 1 per source rule is strict)
- ❌ Put Skills on the homepage hero — kept off explicitly, editor confirmed
- ❌ Try to use Vercel device tokens (`vca_`) in CI — they get revoked
  by fraud detection. Use classic tokens (`vcp_`) only
- ❌ Set Vercel `rootDirectory='web'` AND `cd web` in workflow — path
  duplicates to `web/web`. Either one, not both. We use `cd web` + null
  `rootDirectory`
- ❌ Skip the relevance gate — without it 67 % of high-quality content is
  off-topic (Drug Discovery + Biology AI etc.). This was the single
  largest content quality improvement
- ❌ Use 学发 abbreviation in user-facing copy — replaced with L&D

### Do these
- ✅ When adding a HR-related source, expect classify_hr to auto-place
  the items into the 7-module taxonomy
- ✅ When adding any source, run the full pipeline locally first to
  verify scrape works, then push so CI picks it up
- ✅ Issue 01 is permanent — never modify items bound to it. Even if a
  source rewrites a URL, the snapshot stays
- ✅ Vercel Web Analytics is now collecting. In 4 weeks, real traffic
  data will be available; consider integrating into Top 5 ranking
